Our Story

Wine, Before It Became an Industry

We'd rather keep it real.

Six years ago, a friend of mine told me he was making wine. Ever one to try something new, I decided to give it a shot — drove down to a vineyard and picked the grapes myself. The wine came out so good I had to do it again the next year. Six years later, I decided it was time to share it.

Every vintage starts with grapes I personally select in the vineyard, in the hills of Mateh Yehuda, where clusters are hand-selected to ensure the best quality grapes. We bottle in small batches — numbered, not mass-produced — because paying close attention to a few hundred bottles means we can really stay on top of things.

Lots of Siyata D'Shmaya helps out along the way.

Produced under the hashgacha of the Eidah Chareidis, Badatz Yerushalayim.
